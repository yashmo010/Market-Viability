Launch-Blind Product Viability Estimation from 
Specifications via LLM Aspect Bridging 
Rajvardhan Ajitrao Jagtap, Jyotiraditya Abhijeet Shinde, Tanishq Parag Choudhari,  
Yash Ashok Mohite, Yogesh Malakari Honamore 
Department of Computer Science and Engineering 
Rajarambapu Institute of Technology, Rajaramnagar, Sangli, Maharashtra, India 
Abstract—A product team commits its manufacturing budget long before consumers have held the product, yet prevailing empirical 
methods for forecasting commercial reception require signals that materialize only post -launch: customer reviews, star ratings, and sales 
velocity. This paper presents an audited two -stage pipeline for strictly launch -blind product viability estimation operating exclusively on 
pre-launch specifications. In Stage 1, a large language model parses pre -launch technical text under a null -forcing, evidence-grounded 
extraction schema, mapping specifications to a nine -dimensional consumer aspect ontology augmented with technical specification density 
and grounding indicators. In Stage 2, a gradient -boosted decision tree ensemble (XGBoost) with directional monotonic priors maps this 
rich cold -start representation to a review -derived market -success proxy. Under a frozen, forward -in-time temporal evaluation protocol 
partitioned strictly at October 31, 2021 (507 training products, 124 strictly post -cutoff temporal test products), the promoted rich cold -start 
architecture achieves a Spearman rank correlation of rho = 0.672 (95% CI [0.550, 0.762], p < 1e -16) and a top -versus-bottom market 
quintile AUC of 0.971, substantially and reproducibly outperforming the canonical scalar -aspect baseline (rho = 0.562, AUC = 0.890; 
delta_rho = +0.110, paired bootstrap 95% CI [+0.0485, +0.1833], paired permutation p = 0.0004). On genuine cold -start products from 
unseen brands (n = 82), the model achieves rho = 0.633 (AUC = 0.981), demonstrating robust generalization where historical brand equity 
is absent. The complete research artifact —spanning model weights, statistical verification, full figure regeneration, and synchronized 
decision-support application—passes 10/10 automated leakage tests and confirms bit-for-bit prediction integrity under label isolation. 
Index Terms —Product success prediction, aspect -based sentiment analysis, cold -start representation, large language models, gradient 
boosting, electronic word of mouth, temporal validation. 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Commercial hardware development imposes severe 
financial irreversibility: physical tooling, component 
procurement, and manufacturing runs commit capital 
months before the first customer interacts with the 
physical product [1] –[4]. While large enterprises offset 
this risk through expensive consumer focus groups, 
independent hardware makers and small businesses 
routinely commit production capital on intuition and 
unverified market sentiment [5]. When consumer 
reception proves unfavorable, the consequences ran ge 
from catastrophic inventory depreciation to corporate 
liquidation [6]. 
Although decades of econometric and electronic word -of-
mouth (e -WOM) literature demonstrate that online 
reviews strongly govern market trajectories [7] –[12], 
virtually all existing machine learning methodologies rely 
on post-launch signals: accumulating review volume, star 
ratings, or customer sentiment [13] –[18]. At the moment 
capital commitment occurs, however, zero customer 
reviews exist. The predictive task is therefore structurally 
a cold -start problem [19] –[22]: forecasting market 
reception solely fro m pre -launch specifications, launch 
pricing, and category context. 
To bridge this divide, this paper investigates an empirical 
two-stage architecture: utilizing a large language model 
(LLM) as a skeptical semantic bridge that translates pre -
launch product specifications into a structured quality -
aspect representation, followed by a monotonic gradient -
boosted decision tree ensemble (XGBoost) that predicts 
an independently audited review -derived market -success 
proxy. Crucially, we identify a central representational 
bottleneck in prior implementations: compressing 
exhaustive product specifications into nine isolated scalar 
aspect scores discards critical technical evidence (e.g., 
ingress protection ratings, battery capacities, acoustic 
measurements, specification completeness). We propose 
LLM-COLDSTART-RICH, a representation that 
preserves verbatim specification grounding, technical fact 
density, and extraction certainty alongside semantic 
aspect priors.

LEAKAGE CONTROLS

To guarantee that reported performance reflects genuine 
forward-in-time predictive validity rather than 
retrospective overfitting, we institute a frozen temporal 
evaluation protocol strictly anchored to an empirical 
launch cutoff date of October 31, 2021, 23:59:59 UTC 
[23]–[25]: 
1) Training Pool (n = 507): Comprises physical consumer 
electronics products with their earliest customer review 
on or before the cutoff date, drawing from 43,599 
historical customer reviews across five distinct categories 
(Bluetooth speakers, ice makers, power banks, 
smartwatches, wireless headphones).  
2) Launch -Blind Temporal Test Pool (n = 124): 
Comprises products launched strictly after the temporal 
cutoff. The model receives only launch specifications, 
price, category, and brand name. Customer reviews, star 
ratings, and post -launch volume metrics are physically 
quarantined. 
3) Isolated Target Definition: The regression target is 
defined as a composite proxy: SuccessScore = 100 * (0.40 
* vol_norm + 0.35 * rat_norm + 0.25 * vel_norm), where 
normalization bounds are fitted strictly on the pre -cutoff 
training pool [26]. 
 
Fig. 2. Strict forward-in-time temporal holdout evaluation protocol 
with frozen cutoff. 
III. METHODOLOGY & LLM EXTRACTION 
A. Representation Bottleneck in Scalar Aspect Bridging 
In earlier baseline implementations (MODEL_O), the 
LLM compressed arbitrary specification texts into nine 
broad scalar scores (0 –10). Under cold -start conditions, 
this severe compression bottleneck stripped away 
concrete technical differentiators. When two products 
received similar category -anchored scores, the 
downstream regressor lost the technical signals necessary 
to separate competitive products. 
B. LLM-COLDSTART-RICH Architecture 
Our improved representation establishes an evidence -
grounded bridge conforming strictly to pre -registered 
validation schemas:  
1) Verifiable Technical Attributes: Direct technical specs 
(e.g., ingress protection IP ratings, battery life in hours, 
wattage, connectivity generations) are parsed and 
validated against verbatim source phrases, enforcing a 
0.0% hallucination rate.  
2) Specification Completeness & Density: We extract 
quantitative metrics capturing technical specification 
richness: explicit feature bullet counts, total specification 
word count, and concrete numerical measurement density. 
Under new -item cold -start theory, specification 
completeness signals commercial maturity and product 
readiness [27] –[30]. 
3) Directional Monotonic Constraints: Downstream 
XGBoost fitting enforces scientifically established 
directional priors: price enters with a negative monotone 
constraint ( -1), whereas brand tier (+1) and specification 
quality aspects (+1) monotonically aid predicted viability 
[31]–[34]. 
IV. EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS & BENCHMARKS 
A. Primary Temporal Performance 
Table III and Fig. 3 summarize performance across the 
frozen 124 -product temporal holdout. The promoted 
Model_New achieves a Spearman rank correlation of rho 
= 0.672 (95% bootstrap CI [0.550, 0.762], p = 1.25e -17) 
and top -versus-bottom quintile AUC of 0.971 (RMSE = 
19.75, MAE = 16.66). This represents a statistically 
significant improvement of delta_rho = +0.110 over the 
reproduced canonical baseline Model_O (rho = 0.562, 
AUC = 0.890, RMSE = 21.12; paired bootstrap 95% CI 
[+0.0485, +0.1833], paired permutation p = 0.0004). 
 
Fig. 3. Predictive performance comparison between proposed model 
and canonical baselines. 
B. Cold-Start Unseen-Brand Analysis 
To rigorously evaluate launch -blind performance when 
brand equity is unavailable, we evaluated the subset of 82 
temporal test products belonging to brands with zero pre -
cutoff footprint (Tier 0). On this pure cold -start cohort, 
Model_New achieves rho = 0.633 (AUC = 0.981, RMSE 
= 20.90), substantially outperforming Model_O (rho = 
0.516, AUC = 0.871, delta_rho = +0.117). This confirms 
that preserving specification density and verifiable 
attribute evidence directly resolves new -item cold -start 
ambiguity.

V. ABLATION & ROBUSTNESS AUDITS 
A. Controlled Ablation Analysis 
A controlled feature ablation across configurations A0 
through A7 (Fig. 6) isolates the causal drivers of 
improvement. Removing brand equity (A5) drops 
correlation to rho = 0.609, confirming that brand provides 
an orthogonal launch-time signal. Removing specification 
density (A2) drops correlation to rho = 0.633, whereas 
Model_New (A3, rho = 0.672) achieves optimal trade -off 
between semantic abstraction and technical completeness. 
 
Fig. 6. Controlled feature ablation study evaluating component 
contributions. 
B. Seed Stability & Byte-for-Byte Integrity 
Across five random seeds (42, 123, 456, 789, 999), 
Model_New exhibits stable outperformance (mean rho = 
0.633 + - 0.033 vs baseline mean rho = 0.583 + - 0.025). 
Furthermore, our automated leakage test suite confirms 
100% compliance across all 10 criteria. An independent 
byte-for-byte integrity test —running prediction with test 
ground truth present versus deleted —yielded bit -for-bit 
identical hashes (SHA-256: 3f75df2ebaf508eb...), proving 
zero test label leakage.

VI. DISCUSSION, ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS & 
LIMITATIONS 
A critical scientific distinction must be maintained: the 
target predicted by this system is a review -derived 
market-success proxy, NOT direct unit sales, dollar 
revenue, or commercial profit. While consumer 
satisfaction and review velocity strongly correlate with 
commercial viability in e -commerce markets [35] –[40], 
unmodelled factors such as paid advertising spend, 
distributor relationships, and supply chain logistics 
influence aggregate sales volume. Furthermore, 
survivorship bias is inherent to public r eview platforms: 
products with fewer than five reviews were excluded 
during corpus curation. Consequently, predictions 
evaluate expected consumer satisfaction and engagement 
conditional on achieving baseline market visibility.

VII. CONCLUSION 
We have demonstrated that the semantic compression 
bottleneck in launch -blind product success prediction can 
be legitimately resolved by augmenting LLM aspect 
bridging with evidence -grounded cold -start 
representations. On a frozen temporal holdout of 124 
physical products, the promoted architecture achieves rho 
= 0.672 and AUC = 0.971, delivering a reproducible, 
statistically significant improvement (delta_rho = +0.110, 
p = 0.0004) without altering target definitions, temporal 
partitions, or downstream lear ning models. Under pure 
cold-start conditions, unseen -brand ranking correlation 
reaches rho = 0.633. The complete research package, 
synchronized application inference engine, and audit 
records provide an open, verifiable foundation for pre -
production hardware intelligence.
